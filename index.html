02 / A LITTLE ABOUT US

More than a coffee.
A corner of your day.

Café Bliss began with two friends, a coffee cart, and a simple idea: a good cup of coffee tastes even better when you have somewhere to enjoy it.

That cart became a neighborhood café. The tables got bigger, the menu grew, and the regulars became friends. But the idea stayed the same.

We take our time with the small things. Beans carefully brewed. Food prepared fresh. Ingredients from nearby growers whenever possible. A proper welcome, every time.
