Our Story

Founded in 2010, Café Bliss began as a small coffee cart with a big dream - to create a community space where people could connect over exceptional coffee and food. What started as a passion project between two friends has grown into a beloved neighborhood institution.

We pride ourselves on sourcing the finest organic, fair-trade beans and partnering with local bakeries and farms to bring you fresh, seasonal ingredients. Every cup we serve is crafted with care, and every bite tells a story of quality and passion.
